## Assumptions & Model

**How Follomax makes money:**
You buy services from ExoBooster at wholesale rates and resell them at a markup to your customers.
A typical SMM panel operates on a **30–60% gross margin** — meaning if a service costs you ₦100 from ExoBooster, you charge the customer ₦130–₦160.

**Growth model used:**
- Realistic slow start (no viral marketing assumed)
- Growth driven by word of mouth, social media promotion, and referral/merchant program
- Nigerian market focus (NGN pricing, local payment via Flutterwave)
- Average order value: ₦2,500
- Average orders per active user per month: 4
- Customer acquisition assumed through organic social media + referrals

**Three scenarios modelled:**
- Conservative — minimal marketing, slow organic growth
- Realistic — moderate promotion (social media, referrals)
- Optimistic — active marketing + merchant network growing fast

## Key Growth Levers

These are the actions that will most directly move your numbers:

### 1. Merchant/Reseller Program
Onboarding even 3–5 active merchants who resell your services to their own clients
can multiply your order volume by 3–5x without you acquiring each customer individually.
Merchants take a smaller margin, you take volume. This is the single biggest lever.

### 2. Social Media Presence
A Twitter/X, Instagram, or TikTok account showing proof of results (order completions,
balance screenshots, testimonials) builds trust fast in the Nigerian market.
Aim for 2–3 posts per week in the first 3 months.

### 3. WhatsApp/Telegram Groups
Nigerian SMM buyers congregate in WhatsApp and Telegram groups.
Dropping your panel link in relevant groups (with mod permission) can drive
100–300 sign-ups in a single day.

### 4. Referral Incentives
Activate the merchant referral system built into the platform. Offering ₦500–₦1,000
wallet credit per referred paying user creates a self-sustaining acquisition loop.

### 5. Pricing Strategy
Check competitor panels (SMMFollows, Peakerr, JustAnotherPanel) and price
10–15% below them on your top 5 most popular services. Win on price first,
then build loyalty on reliability and UX.

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |

## Realistic First-Week Expectation

Be honest with yourself: **Week 1 will be slow.**

Most panels do ₦30,000–₦80,000 in their first week, purely from friends, family,
and the first batch of users you manually invite. This is normal.

The inflection point for most panels is **Month 2–3** when word-of-mouth kicks in
and the first merchants start referring users. If you reach 100 active users by
Month 3, you are on the realistic trajectory above.

**Focus for the first 30 days:**
- Get 5 real people to place real orders and screenshot the results
- Share those results publicly
- Onboard 1–2 merchants who already have a social media client base
- Reply to every support message within 1 hour — reputation is everything early on
